ThatFlag Privacy Policy

Effective: September 18, 2026 · Operator: Indie-Hackerz (ThatFlag)

ThatFlag helps you drop a flag at your current location and walk back to it. This English page summarizes the same practices described in the Korean policy used for Korean store/compliance disclosure.

1. Purpose

Saving flags and finding them (distance and compass guidance).

2. Data categories

  • Personal location data: coordinates and accuracy
  • Flag name/notes and optional photos
  • Permission state for location/photos

No accounts, advertising IDs, analytics SDKs, or uploads to our servers. No cookies/ad-ID style automatic collection by us.

3. Retention

  • Kept on-device in the app until you delete the flag, reset local data, or uninstall.
  • No server-side retention period, because we do not host user data.

4. Location and background use

  • Location is used only to save/find flags.
  • While Find is active, background location may continue off-screen and may appear on lock screen, Live Activity, or an ongoing notification. Stopping Find ends that use.
  • Because there is no server, we do not create or retain “records of collection/use/provision of personal location information” on our side.

5. Third parties and processors

  • Core finding works offline with on-device sensors.
  • Only if you choose “Open in Maps” may coordinates go to a maps app/website under that provider’s policy.
  • No other third-party provision, processing outsourcing, sale, or rental.

6. Pseudonymized / sensitive data

Not applicable. Optional photos are user-added content for flag notes.

7. Deletion

  • Deleting a flag removes its photo, note, and coordinates from app storage.
  • Uninstall or local reset removes locally stored app data.
  • No server purge procedure, because there is no server dataset.

8. Your choices

  • View/delete flags in-app
  • Change permissions in system settings
  • Reset local data or uninstall to remove all local app data

9. Security measures

On-device storage, no transmission to our servers, and backup exclusion where supported. Relies on the device OS and store security model.

10. International transfers

We do not transfer user data to our servers abroad or collect it directly abroad. Opening Maps may send coordinates to a maps provider under its policy.

11. Children

Not directed at children; we do not knowingly collect children’s personal or location data. Guardian-consent flows are not applicable.

12. Changes

Material changes may require in-app re-consent and/or an update here. This page is published at a public URL (e.g. Cloudflare Pages).

13. Contact

Privacy/location inquiries: open an issue on Indie-Hackerz/thatflag. A named contact email may be added later.

This page is a public store disclosure and is not a substitute for legal advice.
