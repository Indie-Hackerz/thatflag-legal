ThatFlag Privacy Policy

Effective: September 18, 2026 · Operator: Indie-Hackerz (ThatFlag)

ThatFlag helps you drop a flag at your current location and walk back to it. This policy explains what information the app handles.

1. Data we process and why

  • Location (coordinates and accuracy): saving flags and finding them (distance and compass)
  • Flag name, notes, and optional photos: identifying and remembering flags
  • Permission state: enabling location/photo features

We do not currently use accounts, advertising IDs, analytics SDKs, or uploads to our servers.

2. Local-only storage

  • Data stays on this device inside the app and is not sent to our servers.
  • It is excluded from iOS/Android device and cloud backups where supported.
  • A local crash-recovery copy may remain inside the app until you reset local data or uninstall.

3. Background location

While Find is active, the app may use background location even when not on screen, and may show distance/direction on the lock screen, Live Activity, or an ongoing notification. Stopping Find ends that location use.

4. Third parties

  • Core finding works offline with on-device sensors.
  • Only if you choose “Open in Maps” may saved coordinates be passed to a maps app or maps website. Their terms and privacy policies then apply.
  • We do not sell or rent your data.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Deleting a flag removes its photo, note, and coordinates.
  • Uninstalling the app or resetting local data removes locally stored app data.
  • There is no server-side user dataset to purge.

6. Choice and withdrawal

Without precise location permission, core flag save/find features will not work. You can change permissions in system settings and remove data by deleting flags, resetting local data, or uninstalling.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect children’s personal data.

8. International transfers

We do not upload your data to our servers. Opening Maps may send coordinates to a maps provider that processes data under its own policy.

9. Changes

Material changes may require in-app re-consent and/or an update to this page. The effective date is shown above.

10. Contact

Privacy questions: open an issue on Indie-Hackerz/thatflag. A contact email may be added here later.

This page is a public store disclosure and is not a substitute for legal advice.
